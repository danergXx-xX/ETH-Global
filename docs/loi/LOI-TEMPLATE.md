## Statement of intent

The above-named DAO contributor expresses interest in evaluating AI Treasury Council as a treasury decision-support tool for [DAO name] once the project reaches a maturity level suitable for pilot use.

The expected use case is augmenting (not replacing) human delegate decision-making with multi-agent AI debate, source-cited reasoning, and on-chain audit trail of analysis.

This expression of interest is exploratory and non-binding. It does not constitute a contract, a financial commitment, an endorsement on behalf of the DAO as a whole, or a guarantee of pilot deployment.

---

## Scope of interest (3 specific dimensions)

The signing contributor sees potential value in evaluating AI Treasury Council for:

1. **Multi-perspective analysis of treasury proposals** - having 5 specialized AI agents (Bull, Bear, Risk, Tech, Sentiment) debate proposals in parallel, with each agent surfacing arguments a single human reviewer might miss.

2. **Source-attributed reasoning** - every AI claim citing its source (RSS feeds, CoinGecko, DefiLlama, governance forums) with confidence weights, making AI analysis auditable rather than black-box.

3. **Immutable audit trail of analysis** - full debate transcript stored on 0G Storage (decentralized), retrievable by content ID, so the reasoning behind any executed treasury decision is permanently inspectable.

---

## What this LOI is NOT

To make expectations clear:

- This is **not a pilot deployment commitment**. Actual pilot deployment requires DAO-level governance discussion and approval.
- This is **not a financial commitment** of any kind (neither party owes the other anything).
- This is **not an endorsement on behalf of the DAO**. The signer represents only their personal perspective as a contributor.
- This is **not a guarantee of confidentiality** beyond standard professional courtesy.
- This is **not exclusivity**. The signer may evaluate any other tool. The project may seek LOIs from other DAOs.
- This **may be referenced** in the ETHGlobal submission, demo video, and post-hackathon communications, with the signer's handle and DAO affiliation only (no other personal data).
